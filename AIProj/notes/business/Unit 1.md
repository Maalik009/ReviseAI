## Measurement Of Profit

- Revenue
    - The total income generated by a business from selling goods or services to customers
    - Revenue = Price x Quantity sold
- Fixed Costs
    - Expenses that do not vary with the level of production
    - Examples :
    
      - Rent
    
      - Salaries
    
      - Permanent staff wages
    
      - Insurance
    
- Variable Costs
    - Expenses that fluctuate with changes in a company’s level of production
    - Total variable costs = Variable cost per unit  x  Total units of output
    - Examples :
    
      - Raw materials
    
      - Packaging
    
      - Direct labour wages
    
      - Delivery expenses
    
- Total Costs
    - Total Costs = Fixed costs + Variable costs
- Profit
    - Profit = Total revenue  - Total costs
    - When value is negative it is a loss and when positive it is a gain

## Mission & Objectives

- Mission
    - A mission statement is a brief qualitative statement that summarizes the purpose and core values of a business
- Corporate Aims
    - Provides guidance for setting objectives and are more long-term goals a company wants to achieve
- Corporate Objectives
    - These are more specific and measurable goals to guide its operations and strategic direction

## Limited vs Unlimited Liability

- Unlimited Liability
    - Business owners are personally responsible for all debts, as the business and owner are legally the same entity, putting personal assets at risk
    - Sole trader
- Limited Liability
    - Business owners are only responsible for business debts up to their investment, as the business is a separate legal entity, protecting personal assets
    - Private Limited Company, Public Limited Company

## Business Forms

- Sole Trader
    - An individual who owns and operates a business alone, without shareholders, and has unlimited liability
    - Advantages :
    
     - Complete control over the business and the ability to make all decisions
    
      - Simplicity of starting and running with minimal paperwork
    
      - All profits go directly to the sole trader
    
- Private Limited Company (Ltd)
    - A private limited company is a privately owned business where shareholders have limited liability, and shares can only be bought and sold privately
    - Advantages :
    
     - Limited liability
    
      - Ability to raise capital by issuing shares
    
      - Perceived credibility and reputation over sole traders
    
- Public Limited Company (Plc)
    - A business structure where the company is a separate legal entity from its owners, and its shares are publicly traded on a stock exchange
    - Advantages :
    
     - Raise large amounts of finance through selling shares to the public
    
      - Gains more publicity
    
      - Perceived credibility and reputation
    
- Non-Profit Organizations
    - Aim to fulfill a social objective and make money through fundraising activities
- Social Enterprises
    - A business that prioritizes social or environmental goals while also generating profit

## Private vs Public Sector

- Private Sector
    - Consists of businesses owned by individuals or companies, not the government, and typically aims to make a profit
- Public Sector
    - Consists of organizations owned, funded and operated by the government to provide public services

## Shares & Share Price

- Shares
    - Units of ownership in a company, representing ownership in privately limited companies or publicly limited companies
    - PLCs will have more shareholders than LTDs as they are listed on the public stock exchange
- Shareholders
    - Shareholders make money through dividends (a share of the company’s profits) and capital gains (the profit made from selling shares at a higher price than they were purchased)

### Change In Share Price

- Share price increases if there are more buyers than sellers
- Share price decreases if there are more people selling the share than buying
